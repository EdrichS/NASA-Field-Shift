SOWSMART: USE OF SUPER INTELLIGENCE (SI) / AI DISCLOSURE
NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Challenge: "Adapting Farms with NASA Data"

The 2026 Space Apps rules allow SI tools and ask teams to say clearly where and how they were
used. This file is that statement.

1. HOW THE TEAM USED AI TO BUILD SOWSMART

   The idea, the choice of problem, the product decisions (who it is for, what it recommends,
   which data to trust, what to show farmers) and the final review are the team's.

   Claude Code (Anthropic) was used as a coding assistant, directed and reviewed by the team, for:
   - Code: most of the backend (Python/FastAPI), frontend (React/TypeScript), database
     migrations and scripts. The teammate's screen designs were kept and connected to the data.
   - Tests: the automated test suite (240 tests).
   - Data preparation: the crop-trait table (data/crops_traits.csv) and pest-weather rules
     (data/pest_rules.csv) were compiled with its help from cited sources; every row names its
     source. The country crop table (data/regional_crops.csv) is computed from FAOSTAT by a
     script, not written by AI.
   - Writing: documentation, the project page draft, the demo guide and the slide deck text.
   - Checking: verifying dataset IDs, API parameters and source links against official pages.

2. HOW AI IS USED INSIDE THE APP

   - Grok (xAI) writes the plain-language reports (Tier 1 summary and Tier 2 follow-up answers)
     from the scoring engine's results, in the farmer's chosen language(s).
   - Grok never decides. A rules-based Python engine computes every recommendation and score
     from NASA and partner data. Grok only explains them and cannot change the ranking.
   - Every number in an AI report is checked against the engine's output. A report with a
     number that isn't in the data is retried once, then the unsupported sentence is removed.
   - AI-written text is labeled "Written by AI from SowSmart's results" in the app and in the
     PDF. Alert messages are fixed templates, not AI.
   - Reports in languages other than English are marked as less reliable in the app.

3. IMAGES, VIDEO AND AUDIO

   - No AI-generated images, video or audio are used anywhere (app, deck or project page).
   - All visuals are screenshots of our app, maps from NASA GIBS and Esri, and charts our code
     made from NASA data.
   - No NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers are used or altered.

4. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR USERS

   SowSmart supports farmer judgment and local agricultural expertise; it never replaces them.
   Every report ends with questions for a local expert.
